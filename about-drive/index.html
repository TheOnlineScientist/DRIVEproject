About DRIVE

An EU H2020 project

DRIVE is an EU H2020 Research and Innovation Actions (RIA) project on the role of social exclusion in perceptions and realities of extremism in north-western Europe.

5 countries


6 universities


23 members


A team of cross-disciplinary researchers, based in different countries

The DRIVE project consists of a consortium of 23 members, affiliated with 6 universities and 2 civil society organisations and are based in 5 countries across North-West Europe.

Meet our team

DRIVE

DRIVE explores the role of social exclusion and polarisation in north-western Europe. It was led by Prof. Dr. Tahir Abbas at the Leiden University Institute of Security and Global Affairs in The Hague.
