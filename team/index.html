DRIVE

DRIVE explores the role of social exclusion and polarisation in north-western Europe. It was led by Prof. Dr. Tahir Abbas at the Leiden University Institute of Security and Global Affairs in The Hague.
This website was archived on November 2026 and is no longer maintained. The content is preserved for historical purposes and reflects the state of the DRIVE project as of its last update. Some information may be outdated, links may no longer work, and no further updates or corrections will be made. New updates and publications are published at www.tahir-abbas.com/drive-project/. For inquiries about the project, please refer to European Commission or consult the original publications. Use this resource with caution, as it may not represent current developments.
